    Beach Mission 2026 is over, and we're already looking forward to Beach

Everyone is welcome, and activities are free (apart from a contribution for the outing).     Children and young people are welcome to attend whichever sessions they wish - we don't need to know in advance which sessions they will be coming to.


    **All children and young people must be registered before attending their first session - our registration form will be available in June 2027**

about:
  title: What Is Beach Mission?
  content: >-
    The Port St Mary SU Beach Mission was founded in 1901 and is a
    well-established event with excellent support from local authorities and
    churches. Since the decline of tourism in the area, the mission is now
    attended almost entirely by local children and young people, some of whom
    travel from other parts of the island. Around 200 children and young people
    attend daily to enjoy activities including games, crafts, drama, interactive
    Bible teaching, music and outings.


    T﻿he Beach Mission is run by Scripture Union Ministries Trust (SUMT) who work with children and young people across the Isle. of Man
